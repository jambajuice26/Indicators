Indicators are diagnostic, not predictive. A trigger means a branch became materially more likely, not that it will occur. The inference engine is deterministic and rule-based: it reports what the selected combination implies under the model's assumptions, not an independent forecast. Cross-theatre correlation carries more weight than any single trigger. Source reliability and access tier are the analyst's call at the point of collection.
